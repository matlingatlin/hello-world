# Agents that failed, and why

Negative examples on purpose. A filled-in *good* example is a near-domain stimulus
and gets reproduced — measured: subjects copied an example's features even when the
brief forbade them, straws appearing in 17% of designs where the brief said no
straws. Templates constrain shape safely; worked exemplars transmit content.

So this file shows shapes to recognise, never a model answer to imitate.

---

**The surface granted by omission.** `tools:` left out of the frontmatter. It reads
like restraint and means *inherit everything*. The agent that was supposed to read
could push, delete and spawn.

**The decorative wall.** A path-scoped write gate, and `Bash` in the same tool list.
Every deny is one `echo >` away from irrelevant. A boundary is only as narrow as
the widest tool.

**The self-widening builder.** An agent allowed to write `.claude/hooks/` or
`.claude/settings.json`. It can delete the file that constrains it. Whatever else
it is denied is now advisory.

**The persona standing in for content.** *"You are a principal engineer with twenty
years of experience."* Measured negative for correctness — 162 personas over 2,410
questions, largely random; MMLU 71.6% → 66.3% in a second study. What twenty years
actually buys is the list of ways things fail, and a list is content, not costume.

**The rule that is a wish.** *"Never edit files outside docs/."* Warnings failed in
three studies and backfired in a fourth; eight anchoring-warning variants were all
indistinguishable from no warning. If it must not happen, it is a hook.

**The step that ends in a thought.** *"Consider the failure modes."* Raising a
consideration recovers a fraction of the gap; thinking harder recovers almost none.
Every step ends in a number, a `file:line`, a row, or a named module.

**The undifferentiated bucket list.** *"Sort findings into keep / add / remove."*
This is the checklist condition, which measured **no better than no method at all**.
What worked, at ~35%, was different agents each running a different procedure
hunting a different class.

**The green number alone.** *"12/12 passed."* Says nothing about what the suite
cannot see. Report the blind spots with the score, and how many findings were
verified against the artefact rather than taken on a report's word.

**The author who graded itself.** The most expensive one, because it looks like
diligence. Independent testers found 81 defects in this repo's library; that is the
size of the gap self-grading hides.

**The rules that never arrived.** `.claude/rules/` in an agent's design. Measured:
they do not reach a subagent, scoped or not. The agent runs without them and
nothing reports their absence.
